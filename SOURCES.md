# Historical sources and confidence log

Rule used throughout: **historical text** and **gameplay numbers** are separate.
Every historical entry in `data/*.json` has a `confidence` tag:

- **HIGH**: broadly agreed in the scholarship.
- **MEDIUM**: commonly stated; details or dates vary by account.
- **UNCERTAIN**: disputed, thinly documented, or a gameplay assumption.

## Source pointers (bibliographic, NOT yet verified page by page)
These are real works used as starting points. No page-level citations have been checked.
Before treating any entry as verified, check it against the books below and record the page.

- Gordon, Stewart. *The Marathas 1600-1818* (New Cambridge History of India, 1993).
- Sarkar, Jadunath. *Shivaji and His Times*.
- Mehendale, Gajanan B. *Shivaji: His Life and Times*.
- Eaton, Richard M. *A Social History of the Deccan, 1300-1761* (Cambridge, 2005).

## Known disputes flagged in the data
- Shivaji's birth year (1627 vs 1630).
- Date and manner of Torna's acquisition (c. 1646-47; force or arrangement).
- Whether and when Kondana was held and returned (c. 1649 tradition tied to Shahaji's release).
- Sequence of events around Purandar (c. 1648).
- Chakan's holder in 1646-49; Firangoji Narsala's role then (best documented at Chakan in 1660).
- Pune's status: it was part of a jagir held within the Adil Shahi system, so "player-owned" is a simplification.

## Added in 0.4 (1659 campaign)
- Raigad as principal seat from the mid-1650s; Javli taken 1656; Kalyan region 1657; Pratapgad built mid-late 1650s: MEDIUM (commonly stated, dates vary).
- Afzal Khan's 1659 expedition: well attested. The November 1659 events at Pratapgad: heavily shaped by later tradition. The game does not depict the meeting; it uses an abstract diplomatic choice.
- Panhala late 1659 and Siddi Jauhar's 1660 siege: MEDIUM. Siddi Jauhar's arrival is moved earlier for gameplay.
- Netaji Palkar's role in 1659 is uncertain; his placement is a gameplay assumption.
- "Satara Uplands" and the Konkan strips are abstract regions, not precise geography.
- Events (both campaigns) are gameplay illustrations tied to tradition; their effects are not records.

## Added in 1.0 (Era II and III)
Everything below needs a page-level verification pass before being treated as reliable.
- Era II: Shahu 1707-1749; Baji Rao I Peshwa 1720-1740; Palkhed Feb 1728 and Mungi-Shevgaon; Nizam's autonomy c. 1724; Bhopal/Doraha Jan 1738 and Malwa; a decade is compressed into 20 turns. Territory borders and which faction holds which fort are gameplay abstractions.
- Chimaji Appa and Malhar Rao Holkar placements are gameplay assignments. "Mughal Governor (generic)" is a fictional placeholder.
- Era III: Khadki 5 Nov 1817; Sitabuldi Nov 1817; Mahidpur 21 Dec 1817; Koregaon Bhima 1 Jan 1818 (neutral treatment: interpretations differ); Ashti Feb 1818 (Bapu Gokhale killed); Baji Rao II's surrender June 1818. Fort-fall dates (Sinhagad, Purandar, Raigad, Satara) are given loosely as 1818 and tagged MEDIUM.
- "Company Field Commander (generic)" is a fictional placeholder. John Malcolm's gameplay placement at the northern frontier is an assumption.
- Merging the Peshwa, Bhonsle and Holkar into one faction is a simplification; they historically acted separately.
- The Era III "survive" victory is counterfactual; history records a Company victory in 1818.
- Territories marked UNCERTAIN with no sources (Konkan 1817, Northern Frontier, Satara Uplands, Konkan strips) are abstract regions.
- Sandbox lets you play factions against their historical results; it carries no historical claim.

## Deliberate gameplay assumptions (not history)
- Start ownership of every territory, garrison sizes, wall values, revenue, manpower.
- All combat, supply, stability and recruitment numbers.
- The "Bijapur Sardar" commander is a **fictional placeholder**.
- The win conditions (hold four forts at once) are game targets, not documented states.
- Diplomacy rules (truce cost, duration, acceptance) and monsoon effects are gameplay abstractions.
- Turn length (3 months) and unit scale (one unit = a company-sized body) are abstractions.
- No artillery recruitment for the player is a gameplay constraint, not a historical claim.
